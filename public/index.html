✏️ Freestyle
  • Click a block to select it. Click again to go deeper; Ctrl/⌘-click picks exactly what's under the mouse.
  • Press and drag to move it. The line shows where it lands.
  • Double-click text to edit it.
  • Drag any block from the site on the left onto the artboard.
  • On the artboard, drag layers anywhere; they snap to each other (hold Alt to stop snapping, Shift to keep a straight line). Drag the handles to resize.
  • Double-click text to edit it. Arrow keys nudge, [ ] reorder, Delete removes.
